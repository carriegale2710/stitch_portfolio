The assumption under test

This template treats every growth activity as a decision tool rather than a marketing chore. Start with the riskiest assumption, define a measurable threshold, then decide in advance what each possible result means.

hypothesis

If founders see their own feedback grouped with source evidence...

...at least 20% of qualified visitors will request access or join a paid pilot, because the product addresses a recurring prioritisation problem rather than a generic backlog need.

experiment readout

Message tested: “Turn customer feedback into confident product decisions.”

Audience: Solo B2B SaaS founders from targeted community posts and direct outreach.

What I shipped

A guided sample workspace because prospects needed to see evidence attached to a decision before they understood the product.

What I killed

Broad paid acquisition. It created activity but not qualified conversations or pricing signal.

What I parked

Automated Gmail/Discord import until a paid pilot shows that manual capture is the real retention bottleneck.
