01 / opportunity

Problem, user & hypothesis

Early-stage founders capture requests in DMs, calls, spreadsheets, and support messages. The evidence becomes disconnected from the product decision, so loud requests receive more attention than recurring problems.

SignalDesk was designed for founders with fewer than 100 customers who want a lightweight, defensible prioritisation workflow before they need a full support or research platform.

testable product hypothesis

If founders can see source evidence beside grouped feedback...

...they will make faster prioritisation decisions and feel more confident explaining why a feature is or is not being built.

measured by: weekly active use
assumptions to test
  • Founders will capture feedback while context is fresh.
  • Evidence links increase trust in a cluster.
  • Manual capture is sufficient for the first release.

design principle
Keep the evidence attached. AI may organise signals, but it cannot become the source of truth.
Derived principle
synthesis → product decision

Instead of building a broad ticketing system, the MVP centred on a single workflow: capture feedback → attach evidence → group it under a problem → decide what to do next.

rule 01 / paywall

Value before upgrade

Show the user their first grouped problem before presenting an upgrade. The paywall protects ongoing usage, not the initial “aha” moment.

activation before conversion
rule 02 / AI controls

Suggestions are reversible

AI can propose cluster matches, but users approve or undo each suggestion. Source evidence remains visible in every state.

trust before automation
rule 03 / retention

Weekly decision digest

Send a weekly summary only when new evidence changes a cluster's priority; avoid generic activity-email noise.

signal over engagement

architecture rationale

Row-level security enforces workspace boundaries close to the data. Stripe webhooks update subscription state server-side, while a queue isolates AI grouping from the interactive product flow.

what changed my mind

I originally planned an automated multi-source importer. Discovery suggested the bigger early risk was whether founders trusted feedback grouping, so I chose manual capture and evidence visibility before investing in integrations.

07 / next iteration

What I'd do differently

Test payment intent earlier

Use a paid concierge or pre-order test before building multi-workspace collaboration.

Instrument decision outcomes

Track whether a cluster led to a feature, rejection, or experiment — not just whether users created one.

Expand imports after trust

Build Gmail/Discord ingestion only after users demonstrate a repeat capture habit.
